# Daily annoyance

This is more of a weekly annoyance then a daily but it does make my day to day life more stressful.

I play in a rec adult soccer league on the weekends. The biggest problem that we have is figuring out attendance for an upcoming game. Basically this manifests itself in the captain (usually me), having to send a weekly poll in the WhatsApp group chat, then waiting for the responses. Then depending on the responses I might have to either reach out to get subs, or forfeit, or we're good to go.

For me this annoyance has 2 sub-annoyances. One being the responsibility of remembering to send a poll/answer the poll. The second being waiting for people to respond and being in an area of limbo of weather to get subs or not.


# Interviews

Since this is a more specific and unique annoyance I have to interview people in a similar situation. Basically, people in my league. Both those who are captains and are regular players.


## Questions
1. Do you answer the poll when it's sent or do you usually wait till closer to gameday?
2. Does whether you decide to play depend on who else has already RSVP'd?
3. Do you usually know in advance if you are going to miss a game?
4. If you can't make a game, what do you usually do? Do you find your own replacement, DM the captain, or just vote.
