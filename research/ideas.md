A portal for NYC Footy free agents and free agent teams.

The goal of the web app is to alleviate the strains of free agent teams. This includes people not showing up and having a low number of girls.

- individuals sign in
- players can set up their profile with skill level and sub avaliblity.
- captains access team hub
- players can view the team from player view
- players can list avilablity for subbing.
- poll goes out --> players answer --> if there are not enough players it notifiys the captain --> then the captain can use the subs 'portal' to find other players avalible for subing.
