## Issues/problems I encountered

- **What happened:**
    - Most of my issues came in the begining with setting up the next.js project. I had my repo in a google drive folder and I belive that that was causing some issues.
- **What got me unstuck:**
    - After a while trouble shooting I remembered that having your repo in a synced folder, like google drive or oneDrive, could cause issues. I placed my repo in a non synced folder and it worked.
